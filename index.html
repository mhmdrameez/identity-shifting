Identity Shifting

Cut the old identity. Become the new you. Prove it daily.

For any part of life: gym, study, coding, job search, sleep, money, anything. Change who you believe you are, then prove it with small actions.

Built on cognitive restructuring, identity-based habits and implementation intentions. This is a self-help tool, not therapy. If low mood or hopelessness lasts for weeks, please talk to a doctor or counselor.
